Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Rotville TV mobile application (package world.rotville.channel12) and to the games at rotville.world.

1. Information We Collect

Rotville TV runs entirely offline on your device. The app does not collect, request, store on external servers, or share any personal information, account details, location data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics.

2. Local Device Storage

High scores, sound and vibration preferences, and which channels you have played are saved locally on your device only. Removing the app deletes this local data from your device.

3. Permissions

The Android app requests only the VIBRATE permission to provide haptic feedback during gameplay, which can be switched off at any time in the remote or settings menu. The app does not request network or internet permissions.

4. External Links

The optional "Rotville Elsewhere" screen contains links to the official Rotville pages on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and rotville.world. Tapping one of those links opens it outside the app in your device's browser or the respective platform's app, where that platform's own privacy policy applies.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no personal data or identifiers from any user, no personal information is collected from children.

6. Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy or Rotville TV, write to support@travnikov.dev.
